THE STAKES

This is the rematch. Ole Miss ended Georgia’s season 39–34 in the Sugar Bowl on New Year’s Day, on the way to the program’s first Playoff run. Georgia took the regular-season meeting in Athens last fall. This one is in Oxford, and it will be loud.

Kickoff (3:30 or 7:00) gets announced the week of the game. Bus and tailgate times lock then.

WHAT’S COVERED

✓ Game tickets
✓ Tailgate spot in the Grove
✓ Food + drinks all day
✓ Buses both ways

Your only job: show up in the gear. Swag is encouraged — grab it at the bookstore Friday if you come up short.

THE GROVE

Ten acres of oaks in the middle of campus, tens of thousands of people, and the most famous tailgate in college football. We have a spot in the middle of it from the morning on.
